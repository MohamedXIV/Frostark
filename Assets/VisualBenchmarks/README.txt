V01 visual benchmark source assets

The versioned visual-benchmark.json is tracked. The comparison scenes,
materials, textures and PNG captures are intentionally NOT pre-generated by
an unverified YAML or fake renderer. Use the opt-in Unity Editor generator in
Assets/Editor/VisualBenchmark/VisualBenchmarkBuilder.cs, inspect all generated
assets and .meta GUIDs, and run Unity import/render gates before review.

Do not treat these geometry fixtures as final FrostArk art or gameplay scenes.
