# World, catastrophe, regions and systemic narrative

## World premise

A maritime world is undergoing accelerating freeze. Oceans remain partly open amid pack ice, floes, dead channels, islands, wrecks, abandoned ports, industrial facilities and failing settlements. Temperature, wind and storms, ice pressure and depletion drive ongoing movement. Distant zones may become more dangerous and sometimes more rewarding. The underlying catastrophe is **not initially explained**, perhaps never reduced to a single answer.

An in-world loose term (e.g. *the Haze / the Pale / the Drift*) may emerge, but **no name or cause has been canonized**. The same observations may fit volcanic aerosol, biological catastrophe, fallout, geoengineering, or multiple interacting causes.

## Zone progression (working taxonomy)

Coastal Freeze → Pack Ice → Deep Freeze → Polar Dead Zone. These are climatic/navigation profiles, not a linear quest order. Data determines sea/air temperature, storms, currents, ice distribution, resource density, human activity, contamination, sight distance and entry preparation thresholds.

## Simulated actors

Factions, raiders, trading/rescue parties, scattered settlements, ships, wildlife and anomalous organisms consume/seek resources and create events. NPC actions can happen without player observation; distant actors are coarse-grained, not fully physicalized. Reports, radio chatter and site aftermath are partial observations, not perfect truth.

## Three threat families

- **Nature:** dynamic storms, progressive freeze, disease, starvation and loss of heat.
- **Humans:** salvage competition, raiders with improvised armed boats, uncertain settlements, ethically ambiguous factions.
- **Unknown:** mutated marine/polar life, fungal colonies, engineered specimens, contaminated industrial/laboratory zones, intentional experiments, unexplained anomalies.

Avoid turning every unknown into a generic zombie. Environment tells history through placements, logs, wrecks, scavenged machines and inconsistent accounts.

## Mission and story framework

Distress calls, contracts, personal crew histories, trade, faction grudges and rumors emerge from simulation and authored data. A rumored warm current / convoy / reactor / research facility can become a long-term objective without enforcing a mandatory path. Multiple options may include assist, steal, ignore, negotiate, expose or return later; world state continues between visits.

## System contracts

Each region/site defines identity, seed, environmental profile, access constraints, discoverable content, persistent diffs and exposure/resolution states. Events must identify provenance and time. Procedural generation should produce navigable and tactically readable play spaces, with hand-authored anchors where needed. Seeds are not a replacement for save-state changes.

## Deferred design decisions

Exact weather/ice physics, map scale, region load strategy, world threat progression curve, faction granularity and catastrophic explanation remain experiments/decisions, never implied implementation commitments.
